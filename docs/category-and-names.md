- agentdeals.dev/pricing-changes (https://agentdeals.dev/pricing-changes): of its tagged change entries, `ai-ml` has 122 and `databases` 57. There's no dev-infra or observability category, and none of the vendors in it are observability tools.
- usagepricing.com (https://usagepricing.com): built around how AI/SaaS companies price usage and around LLM token prices. Its Blueprint page doesn't mention Honeycomb at all; Sentry, Datadog and New Relic show up 2 times each.
- Opportunity: observability pricing is complicated (per GB, per host, per span, per seat) and it changes. For example, Logz.io's page announces new Metrics pricing from Oct 1, 2026, and Highlight.io's pricing page now redirects to LaunchDarkly.
