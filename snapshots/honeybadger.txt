Metadata Add custom context data (such as user_id or user_email) to your errors for correlation and debugging.
User feedback Embed a user-feedback form on your error pages. Feedback is automatically attached to the error that just occurred.
Deployment tracking Know who deployed what, when. This feature also integrates nicely with GitHub to show the commits that got deployed.

Custom per-project retention limits Set a custom data retention limit for each project to ensure sensitive data is deleted within a shorter period than your plan's default retention period.

Frequently Asked Questions
What happens when my trial ends? New accounts start with a 30-day free trial, with collaboration features and unlimited users. No credit card required. Before the trial ends, we'll email you to confirm your plan. Add a payment method to keep your plan, or switch to the free Developer plan at any time to keep monitoring your apps as a single user.
If you haven't chosen a plan by then, we'll pause monitoring until you log in and pick one.
Does the free plan include logging? Yes! All plans start with 50MB/day of Honeybadger Insights data. You can upgrade to our paid add-on to send more.
Is application monitoring software worth the investment for individual developers? Yes, especially when you can use Honeybadger for free, forever. Application monitoring can save individual developers hours of debugging by helping them find, diagnose, and fix production issues faster.
Plus, Honeybadger delivers error tracking, logging, performance monitoring, uptime monitoring, and status pages, so you can consolidate all your monitoring into a single free platform.
Why is application monitoring worth the investment for engineering teams? Application monitoring helps engineering teams spend less time investigating production problems and more time building software.
Starting at just $26/month, Honeybadger’s Team plan gives your team shared visibility into errors, logs, performance, and uptime in one platform. Your Team plan also includes higher usage thresholds, unlimited users, and collaboration features designed for small businesses and engineering teams within larger companies.
That means fewer tools to manage, less time spent debugging, and no need to pay for a bloated enterprise observability platform.
What if I need more resources? No problem. We have larger plans available, and we can customize our pricing to meet your needs. Just shoot us an email , let us know what you need and we'll make it work.
Do you accept purchase orders or checks? We accept POs and payment by check for users on the annual plans.
Which languages are supported? Error monitoring is supported in Ruby, JS, Node, Go, Elixir, Python, PHP, Java, and more—see our docs for the full list. Uptime monitoring is supported in all platforms.
How does the limit work? Our goal at Honeybadger is to keep you from hitting hard limits. When you exceed your monthly error limit, we continue to process up to 125% of your limit before we stop processing data until the end of the month. We also send a lot of nice emails along the way to make sure there are no surprises, and you can configure your own traffic limits per-project. You also have the option of enabling overage billing, if you routinely go just a little over your plan's quota. When enabled, you pay $0.0003 (Team plans) or $0.0006 (Business plans) for every notification we process over and above your quota.
Quotas for Insights work a little differently. The quota is daily, not monthly, and we don't stop processing data when you exceed your quota. Instead, you will be prompted to upgrade your account if you consistently exceed your quota.
More details about how quotas work can be found in our documentation .
Do you throttle? We allow for bursts of error traffic without any throttling. We don't routinely throttle (aside from doing so to enforce the limits), but if your project's traffic is sufficient to impact the service we offer to other customers, we will throttle your project on a temporary basis.
How does retention work? Most error data gets stale pretty quickly, so we don't keep everything forever. We purge data based on the retention settings of your account.
Can I pay for Honeybadger through AWS Marketplace? Yes. Honeybadger is available on AWS Marketplace , so teams that procure infrastructure through AWS can subscribe and consolidate Honeybadger charges onto their existing AWS invoice. If you're an existing customer and want to migrate your account to AWS Marketplace billing, email support to discuss options.
