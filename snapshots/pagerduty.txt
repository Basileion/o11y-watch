FAQ
Why did PagerDuty introduce the new PD Reliability Platform offering? Many of our customers are all-in on AI. Code ships faster than anyone can keep up with, but faster isn't safer — things break more, incidents run longer, and AI systems fail by not coordinating with each other. Reliability is now the outcome our customers are buying, and it means two things: recovering fast when something breaks, and preventing it from breaking at all.
How do organizations access and use the PD Reliability Platform offering? Choose a platform subscription based on your operational scale. Each plan includes full access to the PD Reliability Platform with an initial capacity of Seats, Events, and AI Actions built-in. If more Seats, Events, or AI Actions are needed beyond the platform amounts, credits can be purchased to extend platform capacity. Track usage through self-service reporting to evaluate how your Seats, Events, and AI Actions are being used and inform future sizing decisions, while maintaining predictable, platform-based pricing.
What capabilities are included in the PD Reliability Platform offering? The PD Reliability Platform offering delivers a unified set of capabilities spanning incident response, event intelligence, automation, AI-assisted operations, and analytics. These capabilities are natively integrated and designed to work together as a single system. The PD Reliability Platform offering is structured to support people, signals, and automation across the platform, helping to ensure organizations can operate efficiently and effectively.
How can I track my capacity across all of the PD Reliability Platform capabilities? The PD Reliability Platform offering lets you quickly see how much of the platform and your credits you've used — and what's left — in your environment. It refreshes often, and we'll let you know when you cross key milestones so you can stay in control.
Can I use AI capabilities with the PD Reliability Platform offering? Yes, customers with the PD Reliability Platform offering can access PagerDuty's full AI suite to scale teams and build resilience. AI agents handle routine tasks, summarize incidents, and automate operations to free up time for innovation.
Can I monitor Events on my infrastructure using the PD Reliability Platform offering? Yes, customers with the PD Reliability Platform offering can use our enhanced Signal Intelligence to reduce incident noise, accelerate triage, and automate work with event-driven automation across your technical services.
How do Seats work in the PD Reliability Platform offering? Each PD Reliability Platform tier includes a set number of Seats. Need to add more people? Just use PD Reliability Platform credits. Those credits are generic and can be used for Seats, Events, or AI Actions, so you can scale as you need — no extra paperwork.
When do I need to pay for the PD Reliability Platform offering? Payment is required upfront to receive access to the PD Reliability Platform. Once payment is processed, you'll gain immediate access to all platform capabilities, at the amounts included in your purchased plan(s).
Are the platform tier amounts interchangeable across different capabilities? No, the allocated capabilities that come with each platform tier cannot be exchanged or swapped for other capabilities within the platform. They are an integrated part of the overall offering and aren't treated as separate, standalone items. For more capacity and more flexibility, credits can be purchased in addition to the platform amounts included in each tier.
How do credits work across different capabilities? Credits in the PD Reliability Platform offering work across the whole platform so you can spend them where you need them most. Credits are allocated and expire annually. You can use credits to add more of any capability, but once they're consumed, you can't reassign them to something else.
What if my Seat or Events usage needs are between two of the different platform tiers? If your needs for Seats or Events fall between tier limits, you can buy credits to bridge the gap. Credits work across all PD Reliability Platform capabilities, so you're not locked into one capability. This gives you the flexibility to scale your PagerDuty setup smoothly and make the most of your budget.
Is AIOps going away? No. The PagerDuty AIOps SKU will continue to exist and remain available for sale/renewal. Inside the PD Reliability Platform, event intelligence capabilities will be called Signal Intelligence.
Is PagerDuty Advance going away? No. The PagerDuty Advance SKU will continue to exist and remain available for sale/renewal. Inside the PD Reliability Platform, similar AI capabilities will be called AI Actions.
How does Paige relate to Shift, Insights, and Scribe Agents? Paige is the SRE Agent — she orchestrates across the platform for reliability outcomes, serving as a sidekick to the responders doing the work. Shift, Insights, and Scribe are functional agents — capabilities built into the PD Reliability Platform — each doing a specific job well rather than coordinating across domains.
