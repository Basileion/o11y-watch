Alerts and notifications
Email notifications
Email notifications about triggered alerts and alert updates. ilert also lets you acknowledge, escalate or resolve alerts via personalized one-click links in email notifications. No need to login into our app.
Unlimited
Push notifications
Get notified about alerts via push notifications using our mobile apps. Notifications are actionable, so you can acknowledge an alert without opening our app, even from your Apple Watch.
Messenger notifications
Get notified about alerts via WhatsApp or Telegram. Notifications are actionable, so you can acknowledge an alert within the messenger without opening our app.
SMS & voice notifications
Worldwide bi-directional SMS & voice notifications. Acknowledge alerts by replying to an SMS or by pressing a number during a phone call. For many countries, our SMS messages are sent from a local number.
100 account-wide
Priority-based notification rules
Define notification rules based on the priority of an alert. This lets you use more obtrusive alerting methods for alert that require immediate attention and less obtrusive methods for the ones that don’t.
TRUE
Maintenance windows
Create maintenance windows for alert sources or services. A service under maintenance will appear as such on your status page. An alert source with an ongoing maintenance window will not create any alerts.
Support hours
Dynamically set the alert priority based on support hours that you can define per alert source.
Alert actions
Execute custom defined actions on an alert, either manually, or automatically upon alert creation. E.g. create a JIRA issue, post the alert to Slack, revert last commit from a code repository. You can also define conditional for when to automatically execute alert actions, e.g. based on alert fields.
Alert templates
Customize the summary and details of alerts to deliver critical information in a format that resonates with your team leveraging built-in and dynamic template variables from your monitoring tools.
Mute notifications
Silence all notifications during periods when you're unavailable, such as vacations.
Time-based alert grouping
Alert grouping helps you reduce noise by clustering related alerts within a defined time window or by allowing only one open alert at a time per source.
Event filters
Accept events that meet your specified conditions. You can filter events by timestamps, count, or select specific event properties.
Threshold-based event filters
The feature triggers alerts based on event count thresholds. For example, an alert can be triggered if there are more than five events within five minutes.
Intelligent alert grouping
Alerts with semantic similarity (even if their content differs) are combined into one using AI.
Event flows
Visually create workflows and custom routing to automate how incoming events are handled, based on conditions like priority, source, or support hours.

Frequently Asked Questions
How does the trial work?
During our trial period, you get access to our full set of features at no costs. No credit card is required.
What payment options are available?
We offer payment by credit card, SEPA direct debit, and invoice. Invoice payment is available for Enterprise customers and for annual subscriptions of €2,000 or more (Supported countries for invoice payment: Austria, Belgium, Switzerland, Germany, Denmark, Spain, Finland, France, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal and Sweden).
Can we pay by invoice?
Payment by invoice is available for Enterprise customers as well as for annual subscriptions of €2,000 or more, fully automated, with no manual steps required. Invoice payment for annual subscriptions is available for Austria, Belgium, Switzerland, Germany, Denmark, Spain, Finland, France, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal and Sweden, with expansion to other EU countries and the US to follow. For all other plans, you can pay by credit card or SEPA direct debit.
How many monitoring tools can I connect to ilert?
You can integrate as many monitoring tools as you want at no additional charge.
Do you have a demo?
Yes! Please follow this link to schedule a call with one of our sales representatives.
How many users do I need?
Anyone with access to your ilert account can receive alerts or is included in an escalation policy, is billed as a user. For view-only access to incidents, services, and status pages, you need to purchase a Stakeholder add-on, available in Scale and Enterprise plans.
What do I need to use ilert?
ilert is a fully managed software-as-a-service solution. All you need is an internet connection and a modern browser (such as Firefox or Chrome). To use the ilert mobile application, you need an iOS or Android smartphone updated to the latest version.
Can I purchase additional quotas for SMS and voice notifications in the Free plan?
The quota in the plan package is a hard monthly limit. If you reach the limit, you will not receive any SMS or voice notifications until the limit is reset next month. To raise the limits, please proceed with one of ilert paid plans.
Can I get a discount?
All yearly plans cost 20% less than monthly. So, if you are looking for a better deal, consider switching to annual payment.
Is the SMS limit for the Free plan reset each month?
Yes, you have 100 SMS/voice notifications per every month.
Do I need the Inbound Call Routing add-on for ilert to phone my on-call engineer?
Voice call and SMS alerts are included in all ilert tiers. You don’t need the Inbound Call Routing add-on for this.
