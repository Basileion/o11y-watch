Frequently asked questions
Do you offer a free plan? Yes. Our free Hobby plan includes one monitor , one status page with three components , and a minimum check interval of 10 minutes . No credit card required.
Is there a free trial? Yes. Sign in with GitHub or Google and start a 14-day Starter trial from Settings > Billing, no credit card required. If you have not added a card by then, the workspace returns to the free Hobby plan.
Do you offer refunds? Yes. If you are not happy with openstatus, reach out at ping@openstatus.dev within 30 days of your payment and we will issue a full refund — no questions asked.
What billing options are available? All paid plans are available with monthly or annual billing. Choose annual billing to get 2 months free — Starter at $300/year ($25/mo), Pro at $1,000/year ($83/mo), and Scale at $5,000/year ($416/mo).
Can I switch between monthly and annual billing? Yes. You can switch between monthly and annual billing at any time from your dashboard.
What are add-ons and how are they billed? Add-ons are workspace-level settings like White Label , Custom Theme , Email Authentication , IP Restriction , extra status pages , and extra monitors . Once enabled, they apply to your entire workspace. Add-ons are billed monthly regardless of your plan's billing cycle.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately, and billing is prorated.
What currencies do you support? We support EUR and USD . Contact us at ping@openstatus.dev or book a call if you have questions about pricing in your currency.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time? Yes. You can cancel anytime from your dashboard. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, and you will not be charged again.
What happens if I exceed my plan limits? We will never stop your monitors or status pages without notice. If you approach or exceed a limit, we will reach out to help you pick the right plan. Add-ons like extra status pages and monitor packs are billed per unit as listed in the comparison table.
Do you offer custom or enterprise plans? Yes. If you need higher limits, custom regions, SSO, a dedicated SLA, or a custom contract, book a call and we will tailor a plan to your needs.
What payment methods do you accept? We accept all major credit and debit cards through Stripe . For annual plans we can also issue invoices on request — contact us at ping@openstatus.dev .
