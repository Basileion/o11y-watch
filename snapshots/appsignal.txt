The three pricing promises
Never get surprised by an invoice.
We help people build stable and predictable applications. We have the same
mindset about our plans and pricing. No surprises. No shocking bills. No nasty
negotiations. Just a great monitoring product for a reasonable price.
No surprise invoices
Never pay more than agreed upfront. Even if you go over your plan tenfold, we
won’t surprise you with a bill after the fact.
Relaxed upgrade policy
Went over your plan’s usage limits? No problem. We’ll start a 30-day usage
review and only upgrade your plan if your usage exceeds its current limits
during the review period. We’ll notify your organization owners every step of
the way.
Uninterrupted monitoring
We never stop monitoring when you cross your plan limits. And we won’t charge
you for overages. All alerts, all insights, still there.
