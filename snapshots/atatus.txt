Common questions about Application Performance Monitoring plans and pricing
How is APM pricing determined?
APM pricing is based on the number of requests processed. You can monitor multiple applications under a single plan, provided the total request volume stays within your plan's limit.
Are there additional costs for monitoring multiple environments (e.g., staging, production)?
No, Atatus allows monitoring of multiple environments without extra charges, as long as the combined request volume remains within your plan's allowance.
Does Atatus offer a free trial for APM?
Yes, Atatus provides a 14-day free trial for APM, allowing you to explore its features without any commitment.
What happens if I exceed my plan's request limit?
If you exceed your plan's request limit, Atatus will notify you. You can then choose to upgrade your plan to accommodate the increased volume.
Is support included in the APM pricing?
Yes, Atatus includes support in all subscription plans, with priority support available for enterprise plans.
