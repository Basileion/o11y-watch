Is Rootly available on the AWS Marketplace to purchase? Yes, for faster procurement with pre-approved legal, security, and prices that contribute directly to your EDP spend, Rootly is available on the AWS marketplace .
Does Rootly have discounts for startups? Yes, Rootly has a couple programs for startups:
1. If your startup is < 100 employees, has raised < $50m, and has been in business for < 5 years, Rootly has special discounts for you to save up to 50%.
2. If your company is < 25 employees you pay Rootly what you can.
Can I change my plan later? Yes, you can always upgrade your plan or add more users at any time.
What does it cost to use more than one product? Prices are additive. Incident Response is $20 per user, per month and includes Status Pages; adding On-Call is another $20, so both together is $40 per user, per month. AI SRE is an additional product that requires Incident Response, and Enterprise includes it. Talk to us about pricing.
Does Rootly offer discounts for bundling Incident Response, On-Call, and AI SRE? Yes, Rootly does offer discounts when you purchase IR, OC, and AI SRE together. For the best incident management experience we actually recommend it.
Can Incident Response and On-Call be purchased and used standalone? Yes, Incident Response and On-Call each work independently within your stack, and Status Pages is included with Incident Response. AI SRE is the exception: it runs on the Rootly platform alongside them rather than being sold on its own.
Is Rootly AI a set of features, or a platform? A platform. One engine and one chat surface run underneath Incident Response, On-Call, Status Pages, and AI SRE—rather than each product carrying its own bolt-on. It reads your catalog, telemetry, code, and incident history, you can point it at your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini keys on Enterprise, and you can drive it from the API, CLI, webhooks, and MCP.
Is my data secure with Rootly? Yes, Rootly is SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA compliant. See Rootly’s security page to learn more.
How long is my trial? Typically your trial will last two weeks. We’re here to work with you every step of the way so we can always extend it if needed.
