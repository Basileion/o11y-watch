FAQ
What happens to data I filter out with SignalControl? You pay the ingestion rate on it and nothing else.
Is ingestion pricing tiered? Yes, for self-hosted. Tiered pricing on ingestion is available when using SignalControl Edge to ingest over 200B signals per signal type per month.
How does tiered pricing work? The rate per million drops automatically as your monthly volume grows, each time that volume doubles, up to our maximum volume tier. You are billed at each tier’s rate only for the volume within it.
What is an Agent0 Credit? Agent0 Credits are the unit of consumption for Agent0, the autonomous production AI built into Dash0. Every interaction that causes Agent0 to take action consumes credits. Simple tasks, like checking service health, consume a fraction of a credit. Complex tasks, like a full root cause analysis across logs, traces, and metrics, or generating a pull request, consume between one and two credits. Credit usage is visible in real time in your account.
What is AI SDLC Insights? An AI intelligence product that surfaces insights into your codebase and pull requests. Priced per user seat.
Are Agent0 and Darkplane in the free trial? Yes. Agent0 Credits and Darkplane are both available during your 14-day free trial alongside all other Dash0 features.
Billing
How much does Dash0 cost? Dash0 uses consumption-based pricing with no hidden fees. Full cost visibility is built in.
Is there a free trial? Yes. Dash0 offers a 14-day unlimited free trial with no credit card required. You get full access to all features during the trial period.
What is included in free trial? Every feature. Infrastructure, Kubernetes, APM, tracing, logs, web, synthetics, dashboards, alerting, Agent0 Credits, and AI SDLC Insights.
Can I control and predict my costs? Yes. Monthly budget limits, spam filters to suppress noisy data, and a cost forecast are built in.
Does Dash0 usage count against my cloud provider commitments? Yes, generally Dash0 can count against your committed spend, but it differs depending on marketplace, payment terms, and individual contractual regulations. We are happy to check this for you.
Can I buy through a marketplace? AWS, GCP, Azure, and Vercel. Usage generally counts against committed spend, depending on your contract.
