Frequently asked questions
Does Oh Dear offer a free trial?
Yes! We offer a 10-day free trial with all features included. No credit card required to start - just sign up and start monitoring your websites immediately.
Why isn&#039;t Oh Dear the cheapest option?
Because cheap monitoring often fails to alert you at exactly the wrong moment. We verify every alert from a second location, watch your sites from multiple regions, and put every feature on every plan, even the enterprise ones like SSO, two-factor, and unlimited users. You're paying for the night you don't get woken by a false alarm, and the morning you hear about a real problem before your customers do.
Oh Dear maintains a feature-by-feature comparison with other monitoring services .
Is Oh Dear expensive if I monitor a lot of sites?
It depends what you're comparing it to. Per site, we're not the cheapest option. But that one price bundles every check - uptime, SSL, broken links, performance, Lighthouse, DNS, cron, and more - so matching it elsewhere means paying for an uptime tool, an SSL monitor, a crawler, and a Lighthouse tool separately. Every plan also includes unlimited users, so a bigger team never costs extra. Add it up and, for anyone watching more than a few sites, one flat per-site price usually comes out ahead.
Can I change my plan at any time?
Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect immediately and billing is prorated, so you only pay for what you use.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express). In the EU, we also accept wire transfers and many local payment methods like iDEAL, Bancontact, and SEPA Direct Debit through our payment provider.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for new customers. If Oh Dear isn't right for you, just contact us within 30 days of your first payment for a full refund - no questions asked.
What&#039;s included in every plan?
Every plan includes all features : uptime monitoring, SSL certificate monitoring, broken link checking, performance monitoring, DNS monitoring, scheduled task monitoring, status pages, domain expiration monitoring, ping monitoring, TCP port monitoring, unlimited users, and unlimited notifications. The only difference between plans is the number of sites you can monitor.
