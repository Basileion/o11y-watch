Frequently asked questions
Do you have discounts for non-profits /
open-source?
No. The Apache 2.0 Community Edition is free to self-host, at any scale. On our SaaS service everyone pays the same published price. You pay, we keep the lights on.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can cancel anytime.
What are accepted payment methods?
Visa / Mastercard / AmEx. Invoice payment available for
Enterprise customers. We also support payments in Bitcoin . If you choose to do so, please send us an
email to [email&#160;protected] and we will send a lightning invoice.
Do I get a 14-day free trial if I upgrade an
existing account?
If you upgrade from an existing account you are not
eligible for the 14-day free trial. The 14-day free trial only applies when you sign up for a new
paid account.
How do discounts work?
Annual billing is discounted against monthly billing &mdash; the annual price for each plan is shown in the table above. You can upgrade an existing plan to annual billing at any time. Volume discounts are available on high monitor counts, high telemetry ingest, and extended retention; contact us for a written quote.
Is there a minimum telemetry retention?
Logs, traces, metrics, profiles and security events are
billed for at least 7 days of retention. You can keep them for as little as a day, and they are
deleted on time, but a GB kept for fewer than 7 days is billed as if it were kept for 7. Session
replay recordings have no minimum: they are billed for the days they are kept.
What happens to API keys and SCIM if I move to a lower
plan?
API keys need the Growth plan and SCIM provisioning needs
the Scale plan. On a lower plan, requests made with your API keys are refused, and your identity
provider can only remove people through SCIM: adding or changing people is refused until you
upgrade again. Nothing is deleted &mdash; both work fully again as they are as soon as you are back
on the plan.
