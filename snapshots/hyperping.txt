Do you offer discounts?
Yes. When you pay for a year up front, you'll get two months free.
Can I try Hyperping for free before committing?
Yes. Start with a 14-day free trial, or use the free plan with 20 monitors and one basic status page. No credit card required.
Can I speak with someone or have a demo first?
We're here to answer all your questions. Contact us to schedule a demo and learn how Hyperping can help your team maintain uptime and communicate effectively with customers.
Can we migrate from another provider?
Yes, contact us and we'll do it for you.
Can we cancel at any time?
Yes, you can cancel at any time. If you paid for a yearly plan, we'll refund any unused months.
How are logs billed?
By volume, on top of your plan. Essentials includes 5 GB of logs a month, Pro 20 GB and Business 50 GB. Beyond that, each GB you send costs $0.25, with 30 days of search included. We count the uncompressed size of the lines we accept, and never bill rejected lines, seats or searches.
Is Hyperping GDPR compliant?
Yes. All data is stored in the EU in GDPR-compliant DIN ISO/IEC 27001-certified data centers. See our security page for more information.
Do you support Terraform / infrastructure as code?
Yes. The open-source Terraform provider manages monitors, status pages and maintenance windows as code, with import support for existing setups. The API is available on all paid plans.
Is AI included, or is it an add-on?
Included. Every paid plan and trial gets Ask Hyperping , in alpha: 10 questions and 3 incident investigations a month on Hyperping’s AI. Connect your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini or OpenRouter key and both become unlimited, with nothing more to pay us. It is not available on the Free plan or on EU-only accounts.
Does Hyperping work with AI tools like Claude or Cursor?
Yes. Hyperping ships an MCP server , so you can query uptime, investigate and respond to incidents, and manage monitors, status pages and maintenance in natural language from Claude, Cursor and other MCP-compatible AI tools.
Do you offer custom plans?
Yes. If our current plans don't meet your specific needs, we can create a custom plan tailored to your requirements. Contact our sales team to discuss your needs and get a custom quote.
