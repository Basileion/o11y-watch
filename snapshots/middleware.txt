FAQs
Everything you want to know about Middleware's pricing
Is Middleware free to use?
We provide 14 days free trial with unlimited data ingestion.
Are there different pricing tiers?
Middleware offers three tiers: Free trial , pay as you go, and custom. Free trial is to use Middleware for 14 days with unlimited data ingestion for free . Pay-as-you-go is a flat rate for data volume and best to get started. Contracted is for longer term commitments, retention rates and discounts.
How is the data usage calculated?
We measure your data on a daily basis, and per data type and source. For example, a 10GB per day in a 30 day month would be 300GB for the month. Note that this is based on the amount of data actually stored on Middleware, not necessarily everything that's sent to the platform. Meaning you will not be charged for data that you decide to drop by using Middleware's data pipeline.
Do you have discounts for startups and nonprofits?
We do! Contact us to learn more about discount options.
Is Middleware just a wrapper for Otel?
We leverage Otel as a framework for our agents because there is no point in creating another blackbox collector. That being said, we have built many components in our stack that handle the streaming, compression, storage, and querying, and of course a UI that brings it all together. We also support many other data collectors and origins like Prometheus, databases, etc. and are not restricted to type of data.
What are your data retention options?
Our free tier is 14 days, pay-as-you-go is 30, and custom contracts have custom retention rates. Contact us to learn more.
What about data security?
Middleware works tirelessly to ensure the protection of customer data and is committed to continuous improvements to our information security management practices. We aim to ensure the appropriate confidentiality, privacy, integrity, and availability practices are maintained in accordance with the requirements of data security standards as outlined by the SOC2 Trust Service Principles. We ensure that its security commitments are well documented and illustrated to user entities through our website, contract agreement, or in a service level agreement. Read more about our security policies here.
Can I cancel my account?
Yes, you can cancel anytime with the pay-as-you-go option. We keep your data for 30 days from the cancellation date so you can download them.
How much does OpsAI cost?
Error detection is free, allowing you to see all detected issues without any additional cost. For root cause analysis and automated fixes using opsAI, we charge based on token used. You can use above pricing calculator to calculate estimate pricing based on token used.
